## ✨ Features

Generate completely custom, production-ready QR codes without ever sending your data to a server. Everything happens securely in your browser.

*   **📱 Multi-Format Support:** Generate QR codes for URLs, Plain Text, Emails, Phone Numbers, and Wi-Fi Networks (including hidden networks).
*   **🎨 Deep Customization:** Granular control over foreground/background colors, pixel size, and margins.
*   **⚡ Real-Time Preview:** Watch your QR code update instantly as you type or tweak settings.
*   **🛡️ Smart Scan Reliability:** Built-in color contrast calculations warn you if your chosen colors might make the QR code unreadable to scanners.
*   **💾 Smart History:** Automatically saves your 5 most recently generated QR codes to local storage so you never lose your work.
*   **🌓 Adaptive UI:** Full Dark/Light mode support tailored to your system preferences.
*   **📥 1-Click Export:** Download your generated QR codes instantly as high-quality PNGs.
